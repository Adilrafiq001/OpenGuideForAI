# Ethics & Responsible AI Policy

**Disclaimer:** This document outlines the ethical and legal principles guiding the development of OpenGuide AI. It does not constitute legal advice.

## 1. Engagement & Behaviour Design
OpenGuide AI uses gamification to encourage learning, but we reject manipulative design ("dark patterns").
- **No guilt-trip copy:** We do not shame users for losing a streak.
- **No pay-to-win or false scarcity:** All features are free; artificial limits are not imposed.
- **Opt-out:** Users can disable streak mechanics, notifications, and gamification entirely.
- **Mercy mechanics:** "Rest days" and streak freezes are built-in and free.

## 2. Privacy & Data Handling
- **Local-First:** All user data (progress, XP, settings) is stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB) by default.
- **Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK):** API keys for cloud LLMs are stored only in the browser and are never transmitted to our servers.
- **Telemetry:** Any learning analytics must be opt-in, anonymised, and used solely to improve the curriculum.

## 3. Legal Compliance Context
We strive to align with global privacy and AI regulations:
- **GDPR (EU) & CCPA (US):** Minimisation of data collection; right to be forgotten (users simply clear browser data).
- **India DPDP Act (2023):** Purpose limitation and consent-driven data usage.
- **EU AI Act:** Providing transparency regarding AI-generated content and ensuring human oversight in the learning loop.
