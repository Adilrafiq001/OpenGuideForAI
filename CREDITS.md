## 🌟 Foundational Open-Source Ecosystems & Giants

OpenGuide stands on the shoulders of remarkable open-source engineering achievements. We extend our deepest gratitude to the creators, maintainers, and contributors of the following technologies:

### 1. **CPython & Pyodide Project**
* **Project:** [Pyodide](https://pyodide.org/)
* **Contribution:** Pioneering CPython compiled to WebAssembly (Wasm) and Emscripten. Pyodide enables OpenGuide to execute real, unadulterated Python 3.12 with NumPy inside browser Web Workers with zero server dependencies.
* **License:** Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0)

### 2. **Three.js & WebGL / WebXR Community**
* **Project:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (Ricardo Cabello / mrdoob & Contributors)
* **Contribution:** The hardware-accelerated 3D rendering engine powering the **OpenGuide Spatial Holodeck**, cosmic starfields, embedding hyper-space projections, and WebXR immersive spatial gateways.
* **License:** MIT License

### 3. **CodeMirror 6**
* **Project:** [CodeMirror](https://codemirror.net/) (Marijn Haverbeke)
* **Contribution:** The next-generation extensible text editor engine driving OpenGuide's in-browser Python IDE, syntax tree highlighting, vim modal editing, and autocompletion.
* **License:** MIT License

### 4. **DOMPurify**
* **Project:** [DOMPurify](https://github.com/cure53/DOMPurify) (Cure53)
* **Contribution:** Ultra-fast, robust XSS-sanitizer for HTML and Markdown rendering, protecting learners from malicious injections and cross-site scripting.
* **License:** Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0

### 5. **Dexie.js & IndexedDB**
* **Project:** [Dexie.js](https://dexie.org/) (David Fahlander)
* **Contribution:** High-performance, minimalist wrapper around browser IndexedDB, providing local-first persistence for 45 curriculum labs, code artifacts, and cryptographic verification digests.
* **License:** Apache-2.0

### 6. **Framer Motion & Modern Web Animation**
* **Project:** [Motion](https://motion.dev/) (Matt Perry & Framer Team)
* **Contribution:** Production-grade declarative physics-based spring animations powering smooth modal transitions, drawer gestures, and UI responsiveness.
* **License:** MIT License

### 7. **Tailwind CSS & Design Tokens**
* **Project:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) (Adam Wathan & Tailwind Labs)
* **Contribution:** Utility-first CSS framework enabling our 20 WCAG AAA calibrated design themes (10 Dark, 10 Light) across OLED, Nordic, Cyberpunk, and Pure Paper palettes.
* **License:** MIT License

### 8. **Lucide Icons**
* **Project:** [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/) (Lucide Contributors)
* **Contribution:** Crisp, consistent, and beautiful icon set providing high-clarity visual cues across our studio navigation, terminal docks, and interactive controls.
* **License:** ISC License

### 9. **Driver.js**
* **Project:** [Driver.js](https://driverjs.com/) (Kamran Ahmed)
* **Contribution:** Lightweight, vanilla JavaScript engine powering our interactive, non-intrusive 10-step Master Studio Tour.
* **License:** MIT License

### 10. **Lenis Smooth Scroll**
* **Project:** [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) (Darkroom Engineering / Studio Freight)
* **Contribution:** Buttery smooth momentum scrolling providing tactile, cinematic navigation across long-form curriculum modules.
* **License:** MIT License

### 11. **React & TypeScript**
* **Projects:** [React](https://react.dev/) (Meta) and [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) (Microsoft)
* **Contribution:** Declarative component architecture and strict static type safety that ensure rock-solid stability across thousands of modules.
* **License:** MIT / Apache-2.0
